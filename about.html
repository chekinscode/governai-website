About

We built the platform we wished existed when we were the ones getting audited.

We started GovernAI because the choice enterprises faced was binary and wrong: block AI entirely, or let employees route around IT with personal accounts and unaudited API keys. We built the accountable middle path.

OUR ORIGIN

Born from compliance pain.

Our founding team spent years inside regulated enterprises watching AI adoption stall — not because the models weren't good enough, but because no one could answer "who approved this, and can you prove it?" GovernAI is the platform we wished existed.

HOW WE BUILD

Built by practitioners, not theorists.

Every feature on this site is shipped and verified against the actual product — not aspirational copy. When something is a roadmap item, we say so.
