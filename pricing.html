Frequently asked

Do you offer an annual discount?

Yes — annual billing saves 20% versus monthly across all tiers. Your account team can apply it at signup or renewal.

Can we mix tiers across teams?

Enterprise agreements can set per-team model and connector permissions, but billing is org-wide at a single tier.

What counts as a "user"?

Any employee with an active seat in GovernAI, whether or not they chat that month. Deprovisioned employees stop counting immediately via SCIM.

Is the free trial full-featured?

Trials run on Professional-tier features for 14 days, including connectors and SSO, so you can evaluate the whole platform before deciding on a tier.
