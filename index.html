03 · EVERY MODEL, ONE GOVERNED PIPELINE

Vendor-neutral by design — every model, one place to govern it.

  • Direct to OpenAI, Anthropic/Claude, and Google/Gemini, plus OpenRouter breadth and free open models
  • A self-maintaining model catalog — requests are never routed to a model that's been retired
  • A complexity router sends simple work to cheap or free models and hard work to the frontier
  • Streams as you type, with automatic failover if a provider blips mid-response
alt_route

The complexity router

Every prompt is sized before it's sent. A quick reformat goes to a free open model; a board memo goes to a frontier model. You get the right cost for the right job without anyone choosing a model by hand.

Free usage stays genuinely free — because it costs us nothing, it costs you nothing.
