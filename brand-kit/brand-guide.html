Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the dot on every side. Do not scale the wordmark below 90px wide — the dot and letterforms lose crispness sooner than the type looks unreadable. The square mark should not run below 16px (its favicon floor).

The dark-mode accent and semantic colors above are tuned for a near-black background and drop as low as 1.6–3.4:1 contrast as text on white — well under the 4.5:1 WCAG AA floor for normal text. Anytime one of these needs to sit on a white or near-white surface as text or a small icon (not just a large background fill), use the corresponding "light-bg safe" shade instead. These are exactly the values shipped in css/styles.css's [data-theme="light"] block, verified with computed relative-luminance contrast ratios, not eyeballed.

Messaging principles

Established during the headline/tagline review pass on the live site. Apply these to any new copy until a full brand-voice guide exists:

  • Short and parallel beats long and descriptive. Competitors in this category ("One API, every model") use 4–5 word taglines. Trailing dependent clauses ("...your company uses.") are where attention drops — prefer two short sentences over one long one.
  • Lead with tension, not features. The real hook is the villain already named in body copy — personal ChatGPT logins, unaudited API keys — not a list of capabilities. Headlines should invoke the stakes, not just describe the product.
  • "Accountable platform," not "control plane." Retired sitewide as of this pass — too infra-jargon, too close to how every gateway competitor describes itself.
  • No unqualified superlatives. Avoid "the only platform that…" claims without a qualifier. Prefer specific, defensible framing ("built in from day one, not bolted on") over absolute claims that invite a counterexample.
  • Compliance postures, not certifications. HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA, NIST AI RMF are "-aligned," never presented as badges or certifications GovernAI hasn't actually earned (SOC 2, ISO 27001 — do not use until true).
  • Attribute every quote. If it's in quotation marks, it needs a speaker or a clear "this is our analysis" label — never an unattributed pull-quote that could read as a fabricated testimonial.

Component patterns

Conventions established during the review-feedback pass. Apply these to any new card, box, or table component so the site stays visually consistent.

  • Card hover. Every card-like surface (pricing card, quote card, team card, compare card, etc.) lifts translateY(-4px), its border shifts to the accent border color, and its shadow steps up to the "lg" shadow token — all on a 0.32s eased transition. Small chip-style elements (logo chips, team rows) use a lighter -3px lift with the "md" shadow instead.
  • Section dividers. A thin centered gradient line (.section-divider, max-width 640px, fades to transparent at both ends) now separates every major section on all 8 marketing pages, so long pages read as distinct blocks rather than one continuous scroll.
  • Comparison-table marks. "Yes" checkmarks are bold and enlarged (1.3rem, weight 800) in the success color; "no" dashes stay at the muted ink color, no reduced opacity — a lighter-opacity dash was tried and tested at under 2.5:1 contrast in both themes, so weight/color contrast alone carries the distinction.
  • Third-party integration logos. The homepage's "Model Providers" / "Knowledge Connectors" strip shows real vendor marks (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Mistral, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Salesforce, Atlassian, Google Drive, Microsoft 365) each on a small white rounded swatch, so single-color marks stay legible in both dark and light mode regardless of the vendor's own brand colors. These live in /logos on the site itself, not in this kit — they're other companies' trademarks, used only to describe interoperability, and aren't GovernAI brand assets.
